# Japanese Knowledge Graph — Study Modules and Interactions

This document includes only modules directly used for studying Japanese.

## Shared drill interactions

These controls appear across the applicable practice drills:

- Select one answer.
- Select multiple answers where supported.
- Type an answer.
- Record a spoken answer.
- Play or pause audio.
- Arrange sentence chunks.
- Tap an arranged chunk to remove it.
- `Show furigana`
- `Hide furigana`
- `I don’t know—teach me`
- `Check`
- Press Enter to check an answer.
- Use Tab to navigate between controls.
- After answering, review:
  - Correct or incorrect verdict
  - Correct answer
  - Explanation
  - Accepted variants
  - Important distinction or contrast
  - Context example
  - Source links
  - Mastery result
- `Continue`
- `Report grading issue`

## 1. Flashcards

Study interaction:

- View a Japanese glyph, word, grammar point, or English meaning.
- Recall the answer mentally.
- `Show answer`
- Press Space or Enter to reveal the answer.
- Review:
  - Answer
  - Reading
  - Additional details
  - Example sentences
- `Turn to front`
- Rate the recall:
  - `Again · 1`
  - `Hard · 2`
  - `Good · 3`
  - `Easy · 4`
- Press keys 1–4 to rate.
- `Undo [rating] (5 seconds)`

Learning effect:

- The selected rating updates spaced-repetition scheduling.
- The learner rates the recall they had before revealing the answer.

## 2. Kana recognition

Study interaction:

- View a hiragana or katakana character.
- Select its rōmaji reading.
- `I don’t know—teach me`
- `Check`
- Review the correct reading.
- `Continue`

Skill tested:

- Kana symbol → sound.

## 3. Kana production

Study interaction:

- View a sound written in rōmaji.
- Select the corresponding hiragana or katakana.
- `I don’t know—teach me`
- `Check`
- Review the correct kana.
- `Continue`

Skill tested:

- Sound → kana symbol.

## 4. Discrimination

Study interaction:

- View two visually or functionally confusable items.
- For kana, choose the character matching the requested sound.
- For other concepts, choose the item matching the requested meaning.
- `I don’t know—teach me`
- `Check`
- Review the correct distinction.
- `Continue`

Skill tested:

- Differentiating similar symbols, meanings, or grammatical functions.

Study interaction:

- Accepted kana aliases may also be recognized.
- `I don’t know—teach me`
- `Check`
- After answering, review a Japanese example sentence and translation.
- `Continue`

Skill tested:

- Meaning → Japanese word.

## 9. Vocabulary guesser

Study interaction:

- View an English meaning.
- Say the corresponding Japanese word.
- `Record`
- `Listening…`
- `Stop`
- `Try again`
- Review the speech recognizer’s kana interpretation.
- When browser speech recognition is unavailable, type the reading instead.
- `I don’t know—teach me`
- `Check`
- Review the target word and reading.
- `Continue`

Skill tested:

- Meaning → spoken Japanese word.

Technical behavior:

- Uses fuzzy pronunciation matching.
- Speech recognition requires Chrome or Edge.

## 10. Conjugation drill

Study interaction:

- View a Japanese verb.
- View the requested conjugation form.
- Possible forms include:
  - Polite ます
  - Polite negative ません
  - Polite past ました
  - Plain past た
  - Plain negative ない
  - て-form
- `Show furigana`
- `Hide furigana`
- Type the conjugated form in kana or rōmaji.
- `I don’t know—teach me`
- `Check`
- Review the correct form.
- `Continue`

Skill tested:

- Japanese verb morphology and conjugation.

## 11. Particle play

Study interaction:

- View a Japanese sentence with one particle replaced by a blank.
- View the sentence translation.
- Type the particle directly into the sentence.
- Optionally reveal the particle bank as a hint.
- `I don’t know—teach me`
- `Check`
- Review the correct particle in context.
- `Continue`

Skill tested:

- Particle selection in a real sentence.

## 12. Sentence scramble

Study interaction:

- View a sentence translation.
- View shuffled Japanese sentence chunks.
- Tap chunks in the desired order.
- Tap an already selected chunk to remove it.
- Continue until all chunks have been placed.
- `I don’t know—teach me`
- `Check`
- Review the correctly ordered sentence.
- `Continue`

Skill tested:

- Japanese syntax and word order.

## 15. Read

Text-generation controls:

- Select genre:
  - `Conversation`
  - `Japanese history`
  - `Folk tale`
- Select length:
  - `Tiny`
  - `Short`
  - `Long`
- Enter an optional topic.
- Select challenge:
  - `Easier`
  - `Current`
  - `Stretch`
- Select register:
  - `Casual`
  - `Neutral`
  - `Formal`
- Toggle `Use today’s [number] focus items`.
- `Generate`

Reading interactions:

- `Furigana: auto`
- `Furigana: all`
- `Furigana: off`
- `Translations`
- Hover or tap a word to open its explanation.
- Focus a word using the keyboard.
- Press Enter or Space to open a word explanation.
- Press Escape or click outside to close it.
- A word explanation can contain:
  - Surface form
  - Kana
  - Rōmaji
  - English gloss
  - Grammar point
  - Grammar definition
- `Open in graph →`
- View words deliberately woven into the passage.

Library interactions:

- Select a previously generated text.
- Press Enter to open a selected library item.
- `Delete text`
- The selected text can also be used in Listen and Karaoke.

Skill practiced:

- Reading comprehension, vocabulary in context, grammar in context, and
  adaptive furigana use.

## 16. Listen

Text-generation controls:

- Select genre, length, topic, challenge, and register.
- Optionally use today’s focus items.
- `Generate`
- Select or delete a saved text.

Listening interactions:

- Japanese text starts hidden.
- `Play all`
- `Stop`
- Play an individual sentence.
- `Show text`
- Select playback speed:
  - `0.5×`
  - `0.75×`
  - `1×`
  - `1.25×`
- `I’m done listening — show questions`
- Questions also appear after listening to the complete text.

Listening-quiz interactions:

- `EN` — show or hide English prompts and answer translations.
- Select one answer for each question.
- Review immediate correct or incorrect highlighting.
- View the final score.

Learning effect:

- The comprehension quiz is graded locally.
- Listening-quiz answers do not update spaced-repetition mastery.

## 17. Karaoke

Text-generation controls:

- Select genre, length, topic, challenge, and register.
- Optionally use today’s focus items.
- `Generate`
- Select or delete a saved text.

Speaking interactions:

- `Start reading`
- `Stop`
- Read the displayed Japanese text aloud.
- Words highlight as they are recognized.
- The activity advances through the text one sentence at a time.
- After approximately five seconds without progress:
  - A kanji word receives a kana hint.
  - A kana word receives a rōmaji hint.
- `Skip word`
- `Translation`
- `Restart`
- View the completion message after reading the entire text.

Learning effect:

- Karaoke practices read-aloud fluency.
- It is ungraded and does not update mastery.

Technical behavior:

- Speech recognition requires Chrome or Edge.
- Local matching is supplemented by server-side phonetic matching.

## 18. Chat

Scenario controls:

- Select:
  - `Daily life`
  - `Restaurant`
  - `Travel`
  - `School`
  - `Shopping`
  - `Custom topic`
- Enter a custom topic.
- `New`
- Select an existing conversation.

Conversation interactions:

- Type in Japanese, English, or rōmaji.
- Press Enter to send.
- Press Shift+Enter to insert a new line.
- `Send message`
- `Record speech`
- `Stop listening`
- `Voice` — enable or disable automatic tutor-audio playback.
- Play individual assistant sentence audio.
- Receive a streaming tutor reply without inline correction.
- `Delete`

Analysis interactions:

- `Analyze`
- Review:
  - Conversation summary
  - Recurring error patterns
  - Original learner examples
  - Corrected examples
  - Explanatory notes
  - Practice targets
  - Suggested drills
  - Suggested next prompts
- Open a suggested drill in a practice session.

Learning effect:

- Analysis is delayed until explicitly requested.
- Analysis can suggest optional drills.
- Conversation analysis does not directly reduce mastery.

These controls support the study modules during a session:

- `Previous`

- Return to previously answered cards.
- Review concepts affected by the session.
- Open a concept from the session summary.
- `Previous card`
- `Another round`
- `Back to today`

## Mastery behavior

- Validated drill answers update mastery and spaced-repetition scheduling.
- Flashcards update scheduling through explicit four-grade ratings.
- `I don’t know—teach me` records useful evidence and reveals the answer.
- Wrong answers can distribute evidence across related concepts.
- Study recommendations combine:
  - Due reviews
  - New concepts whose prerequisites are ready
  - Current focus concepts
  - Weak spots
- Recommendations progress from recognition toward production and contextual
  practice as knowledge becomes more stable.
- Read is contextual study but does not directly grade mastery.
- Listen comprehension questions are local-only.
- Karaoke is ungraded.
- Chat analysis suggests practice without directly changing mastery.
